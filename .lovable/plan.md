# Make Reeler sound more human

## What will change
- Rewrite the wording on Discover, Library, sign-in, and the home page in simple, conversational language.
- Replace technical or marketing-heavy phrases such as “taste engine,” “slate,” “bullseyes,” “raw material,” and “normalized.”
- Make buttons, empty states, import guidance, confirmations, and success messages clear and direct.
- Keep all features, layouts, and recommendation behavior unchanged.

## Tone
Friendly, concise, and natural—like a helpful movie-loving friend, without forced jokes or exaggerated claims.

## Technical details
Only visible text and page metadata will change. No database, recommendation logic, or styling changes are included.
